### Full Description
PNLE Reviewer 2027 is a dedicated nursing board exam reviewer built natively for iPhone and iPad. It ships with a built-in question bank covering all five PNLE subjects, so you can start practicing the moment you open the app — no account, no internet, no setup.

OFFLINE-FIRST PRACTICE
The app includes a local question bank with hundreds of board-style nursing questions. Random Quiz, Focus Mode, and Timed Exam all launch instantly. Airplane mode? No Wi-Fi? No problem.

5 PNLE NURSING SUBJECTS
• Fundamentals of Nursing including Professional Adjustments
• Medical-Surgical Nursing (MSN)
• Maternal and Child Health Nursing (MCHN)
• Community Health Nursing (CHN)
• Mental Health and Psychiatric Nursing

4 STUDY MODES
• Random Quiz — Mixed-subject review from the built-in question bank.
• Focus Mode — Target your weakest nursing category.
• Timed Exam — Practice under real exam time pressure.
• Challenge Mode — Online sessions with fresh question variety.

DETAILED SCORE BREAKDOWNS
After every quiz, see per-subject accuracy, question-by-question review, and session insights.

10-DAY PERFORMANCE TRACKER
Track daily accuracy, speed, and subject coverage. A Readiness Score tells you how exam-ready you are.

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
See your study streak and readiness score without opening the app.

SIRI SHORTCUTS
Say "Start a nursing quiz" or long-press the icon for quick actions.

COACH NOTE EXPLANATIONS
After any question, tap Coach Note for a step-by-step explanation of the correct answer.
