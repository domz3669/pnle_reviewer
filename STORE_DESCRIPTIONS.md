### Full Description
Prepare for the Philippine Nursing Licensure Examination with an AI-powered reviewer built from the ground up for Filipino nursing graduates.

PNLE Reviewer 2027 isn't just another quiz bank — it generates fresh, exam-style questions using AI so you never run out of practice material. Every session adapts to your weak areas, and after each answer you can request a Coach Note: an AI-generated explanation that breaks down the rationale step by step, the way a tutor would.

WHY THIS APP IS DIFFERENT
• AI-Generated Questions — Powered by DeepSeek and Gemini AI, the app creates unique PNLE-style questions on demand so you're never memorizing the same set twice.
• Instant AI Coach Notes — Tap to get a clear, step-by-step explanation after any question. Understand the "why" behind every correct answer.
• Offline-First Design — Random Quiz, Focus Mode, and Timed Exam launch instantly from a local seed pool. No internet? No problem.
• Daily Readiness Score — A performance index combining accuracy, completion, and subject coverage tells you exactly how exam-ready you are today.

5 PNLE SUBJECTS COVERED
• Fundamentals of Nursing including Professional Adjustments
• Medical-Surgical Nursing (MSN)
• Maternal and Child Health Nursing (MCHN)
• Community Health Nursing (CHN)
• Mental Health and Psychiatric Nursing

4 STUDY MODES
• Random Quiz — Balanced daily review mixing all subjects from the local seed pool.
• Focus Mode — Zero in on your weakest nursing category for targeted improvement.
• Timed Exam — Simulate real exam pressure with a per-question countdown timer.
• Challenge Mode — AI generates a fresh mixed-subject session online for advanced practice.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• 10-day performance history with daily accuracy, speed, and coverage metrics.
• Per-subject score breakdowns after every session.
• Readiness insights that highlight strengths and flag weak areas.
• Session save & resume — pick up exactly where you left off.

DAILY STUDY HABIT
• 4 daily quiz sessions build consistent exam preparation.
• Optional daily task rewards encourage streak-based studying.
• Quick Practice mode for fast review between study blocks.

Built natively in Flutter. No webview wrappers. No subscriptions. No paywalls. Just focused PNLE prep.
